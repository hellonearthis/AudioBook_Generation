# Grill-Me Skill

An interactive alignment and pre-generation interview engine based on the **Design Tree & Decision Frontier** methodology (Matt Pocock / kajili).

---

## ⚡ Why This Skill Exists
Most AI interactions fail because the model guesses your assumptions, or dumps a wall of 10 questions that feel like filling out a tax form.

`grill-me` changes the dynamic:
* **One question at a time:** Keeps engagement effortless and high-signal.
* **Always includes a recommendation:** You can simply say "Yes", pick an option, or steer.
* **Fact-finding is the AI's job:** Never asks you for facts it can look up in your files.
* **Walks the Decision Frontier:** Never asks downstream questions until upstream prerequisites are settled.
* **Psychological Inversions & Director Moves:** Probes hidden subtext to surface counter-intuitive camera, lighting, and blocking choices from `director-toolkit`.

---

## 🚀 How to Use
Whenever you want to stress-test a story idea, character, video prompt, or project architecture, say:
* *"Grill me on my next Uncle Obvious idea"*
* *"/grill-me on matching director moves for my scene"*
* *"/grill-me a darksynth music concept"*
* *"Stress-test this scene before writing"*
